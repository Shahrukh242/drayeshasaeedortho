Pediatric Orthopedic Treatments

Evidence-based surgical and non-surgical procedures tailored to the unique healing properties of growing children's bones and joints.

Clinical Procedures

Our Treatment Modalities

From non-surgical manipulations to advanced minimally invasive operations, we prioritize child safety and rapid recovery.

Clubfoot Correction

Ponseti Method

The gold-standard, non-surgical treatment for congenital clubfoot. Involves gentle manual manipulation followed by weekly long-leg plaster casting, completed with a minor Achilles tenotomy to achieve complete correction.

Non-Surgical Orthopedics

Serial Casting

Applying a series of fiberglass or plaster casts over weeks to gradually stretch tight muscles, tendons, or correct joint contractures. Highly effective for walking abnormalities and early limb deformities.

Emergency Care

Pediatric Fracture Management

Specialized setting of child bone fractures. Because child bones remodel actively as they grow, we focus on anatomic realignment, applying comfortable lightweight casts, and monitoring growth.

Pediatric Trauma

Trauma Care

Urgent management of complex bone breaks, dislocations, and soft-tissue injuries resulting from playground falls, sports impact, or accidents. Designed to minimize stress and trauma for the child.

Specialized Physeal Care

Growth Plate Management

Careful surgical or conservative management of fractures crossing the active growth plates (cartilage zones). Special surgical hardware (smooth pins) is used to protect and preserve natural limb growth.

Deformity Correction

Corrective Procedures

Treatment of limb length discrepancies and bone angulation (bow legs, knock knees). We utilize modern **Guided Growth** plates or osteotomies to allow the child's natural growth to straighten the leg.

Advanced Surgery

Pediatric Orthopedic Surgery

Highly specialized operations including spinal fusion for progressive scoliosis, pelvic osteotomies for hip dysplasia, and surgical bone reconstruction for congenital skeletal conditions.

Non-Invasive Therapy

Conservative Treatment

Non-surgical options including orthotic insoles for flat feet, pelvic braces/harnesses for infant hip instability, posture training programs, and dietary management for metabolic rickets.

Recovery & Sports

Post-operative Rehabilitation

Structured physical therapy programs to restore range of motion, muscle strength, and gait patterns after cast removal or surgery. Designed to get children back to active play and sports safely.
