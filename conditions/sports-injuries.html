Pediatric Sports Injuries

Learn about the symptoms, common causes, and specialized pediatric treatments for Pediatric Sports Injuries.

Overview

As children become more active in organized sports, pediatric sports injuries are on the rise. Because children have open growth plates and developing ligaments, their injuries differ significantly from adult athletic injuries. Common issues include overuse syndromes, growth plate inflammation (like Osgood-Schlatter disease), ligament sprains, and joint dislocations.

Common Symptoms of Pediatric Sports Injuries

If your child is experiencing this condition, they may display one or more of the following symptoms:

  • Persistent pain or swelling in a joint after sports practice or play.
  • Limping or stiffness in the morning or during activity.
  • Sudden pain accompanied by a 'popping' sound or locking joint.
  • Reduced range of motion or instability in the knee or shoulder.

Etiology and Causes

What causes Pediatric Sports Injuries? Pediatric orthopedic research indicates several underlying factors:

  • Overuse: Repetitive training without adequate rest or conditioning.
  • Acute Trauma: Sudden twists, collisions, or falls during competitive matches.
  • Improper Technique: Poor form, unsupportive footwear, or insufficient warm-up.

Advanced Treatments by Dr. Ayesha Saeed

Dr. Ayesha Saeed focuses on keeping young athletes active while protecting their long-term bone development. Most pediatric sports injuries are managed conservatively with activity modification, physical therapy, bracing, and structured rehabilitation. For ligament tears (such as ACL injuries in teenagers) or joint instability, she performs specialized pediatric-specific surgeries designed to protect the growth plates.

Thanks to the active bone growth and remodeling potential in infants and children, early detection allows us to implement highly effective conservative treatments. If surgery is required, Dr. Ayesha Saeed utilizes advanced, minimally invasive surgical procedures that preserve growth plates and accelerate recovery.
